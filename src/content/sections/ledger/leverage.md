---
label: IV. Internal Leverage
title: Giving managers back their review time
---

> Review preparation fell to **3 hours** from multiple days.

Performance reviews should be spent assessing work, not reconstructing it. I built an internal agentic tool that gathers activity from GitHub, Jira, and Slack and turns it into calibrated review packets for engineering managers.

It reduced my year-end preparation from a multi-day crunch to three hours. Peer managers adopted it too, turning a personal workflow improvement into shared management infrastructure.
